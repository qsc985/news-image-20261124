1. **Nvidia brings back Huang as CEO after Trump steps in**
   - Outlet: Reuters, via WDSM / The Register / NPR Illinois
   - Published: 23 November 2026
   - URL: https://wdsm710.com/2026/11/23/nvidia-reinstates-jensen-huang-as-ceo/

   - Also: https://www.nprillinois.org/2026-11-23/nvidia-reinstates-huang
   - Facts used: Nvidia's board voted unanimously on Monday 23 Nov to
     reinstate Jensen Huang as president and CEO, 11 days after he
     resigned over revelations of extramarital affairs, including one
     with a woman suspected of being a Chinese spy; the board said it
     had received "input from senior levels of the United States
     government" and was confident his reinstatement posed no national
     security risk; President Donald Trump dialled into a board meeting
     to argue for Huang's return, told CNBC "I believe my intervention
     was very important", and took credit for the reversal; Nvidia
     shares soared 33% on the news, adding more than $900 billion in
     market value; Huang has agreed to testify before the US Senate on
     3 December.

   - Published: 23 November 2026

   - Facts used: the European Commission on Monday 23 Nov proposed its
     22nd package of sanctions against Russia; Commission President
     Ursula von der Leyen said the package targets some 60 Russian
     banks, all to be removed from the SWIFT messaging system, and

     member states.

   - Published: 23 November 2026
