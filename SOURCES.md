## 4. EU unveils 22nd sanctions package on Russia

- AP, via Netscape / KTWB; Reuters, via Global Banking and Finance —
  23 November 2026
- https://staging.connect.netscape.com/news/story/0001/20261123/ecad86868390f6602b346c70d1fa5757
- https://ktwb.com/2026/11/23/eu-commission-proposes-22nd-sanctions-package-on-russia/
- https://cyprus.representation.ec.europa.eu/news/statement-president-von-der-leyen-22nd-package-sanctions-against-russia-2026-11-23_en
- The European Commission on Monday 23 November proposed its 22nd
  package of sanctions against Russia. Commission President Ursula von
  der Leyen said it targets some 60 Russian banks, all to be removed
  from the SWIFT messaging system, and cracks down on crypto and payment
  networks used to evade sanctions, with a new Crypto Sanctions Task
  Force. More than 70 additional shadow-fleet vessels would be listed.
  Russian teams and athletes would be banned from competing under the
  Russian flag at sports events in the EU, and visas would be denied to
  Russian ex-combatants. The package needs unanimous approval by all 27
  EU member states.

## 5. Factory fire toll rises to 24; about 30 missing

- Reuters, via BusinessWorld / WTBX — 23 November 2026
- https://bworldonline.com/world/2026/11/23/796078/indonesian-authorities-say-24-killed-in-factory-blaze-as-rescuers-search-for-dozens-missing/
- https://wtbx.com/2026/11/22/indonesia-says-24-killed-in-factory-blaze-as-rescuers-search-for-dozens-missing/
- Indonesia confirmed on Monday 23 November that 24 people were killed
  in a fire that tore through a building of the PT Fonusa Agung Mulia
  confectionery factory in Cikande, Banten province, on Saturday
  evening. About 30 people remained missing as rescuers searched the
  unstable, burned-out structure, with 29 confirmed safe (five
  hospitalised). About 300 people were on shift when the fire started.
  Preliminary reports point to an air-conditioner short circuit, and the
  cause remains under investigation. Relatives waited at a police
  hospital for DNA identification of victims.

  23 November 2026

- https://www.jagranjosh.com/general-knowledge/pm-modi-meets-nepal-pm-balen-shah-check-key-takeaways-aid-package-8-pacts-iit-ayodhyajanakpur-train-1820014072-1

  Darchula. Shah's visit was his first bilateral trip abroad since
  taking office in March 2026.
